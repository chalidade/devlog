# Pulau Aru: A President, Paved Roads and a Cast of Voices

Day two of [Pulau Aru](https://pulau-aru.web.id/): episodes 3 to 6, with followers
climbing from about 3,000 to 3,560.

- **Episode 3:** fishing boats that sail out, stop to fish and come back, and land
  that rises out of the sea in three stages to make the island bigger
- **Episode 4:** a traditional market with six stalls and a podium, and the first
  island president, sworn in from the creator panel, wearing a peci and sash
- **Episode 5:** a spiral cloud staircase from the top of the pyramid to a cloud
  island with a co-op shop. Residents queue, climb, and now and then fall off
- **Episode 6: paved roads.** The first version routed a road automatically with
  A* from the podium to every building, preferring straight and gentle slopes. The
  same night it was replaced by a planned layout: one straight main road from the
  podium to the pier, one cross road, short spurs to the rest, and every building
  on levelled ground beside them. Mosque and houses got bigger, closer to human scale
- **Visitor counts** with no cookies and no personal data: anonymous counters for
  visits, unique visitors, players and referrers (TikTok, WhatsApp, Instagram,
  Google), shown in a Pengunjung tab. The privacy policy says so
- **Voices.** The narration moved to ElevenLabs through a [Vigenco](https://github.com/chalidade/vigenco)
  workflow: commit a `lines.json` with narrator and character lines, get back one
  MP3 per line with word timings. Indonesian voices first, taken from the voice
  library when the account had none for a role
- A "how to join" menu explains the request rules, with a link to my
  [Trakteer](https://teer.id/chalidade) page for people who want to buy a request

**Lesson:** a road to everything is not a town. Two planned roads with buildings
lined up along them read better on screen than an automatic route to each door.

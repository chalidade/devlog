# Tools: Three Everyday Tools for Chat and Shopping

Day 13 of the [tools roadmap](https://github.com/chalidade/tools/blob/main/ROADMAP.md),
72 tools in total.

- **Link WhatsApp:** a number in any local format (08…, +62…, with spaces or
  dashes) and an optional message become a "Chat sekarang" button, a `wa.me`
  link and a QR, without saving the contact
- **Kalkulator Persen & Diskon:** X% of Y, what percent, increase or decrease,
  and stacked discounts with a table per step, because 50% + 20% is 60% off,
  not 70%. It reads both 1.500,5 and 1,500.5 from the pattern
- **Mana Lebih Murah:** several pack sizes become one price per unit, the
  cheapest is marked, and weight is never compared with volume

**Lesson:** the tools people use every day are small. Stacked discounts are a
one-line formula that most shoppers still get wrong.

# Tools: Zakat Calculator, Digital Tasbih and Number to Words

Day 10 of the [tools roadmap](https://github.com/chalidade/tools/blob/main/ROADMAP.md):
three more tools in productivity, 65 in total.

- **Zakat calculator:** income zakat (a twelfth of the nisab per month), wealth,
  gold and silver (results in grams and rupiah), and fitrah (2.5 kg of rice per
  person). The nisab comes from a gold price the user types in, nothing is
  fetched, and every step of the formula is shown
- **Digital tasbih:** a big counter with a 33, 99, free or custom target, a
  vibration when the target is reached, Space/Enter to count and Backspace to
  undo one, and a total and round history saved on the device
- **Terbilang:** numbers to Indonesian and English words up to 18 digits,
  including decimals, rupiah and sen, and casing options for receipts and
  cheques. A dot or comma is read as a thousands or decimal separator from the
  pattern of the number

**Lesson:** showing the formula is what makes a zakat number believable. A bare
total gives the user nothing to check against.

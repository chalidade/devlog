# Tools: A Home Page That Is One Search Box

Day 12 of [tools](https://chalidade.github.io/tools/): two finance calculators in the
morning and a new front page in the evening.

- **Kalkulator Cicilan and Kalkulator Gaji Bersih:** loan instalments, and
  take-home pay after BPJS and PPh 21. The rates live in their own file
  (`rates.ts`) so a change in the rules is a one-line edit
- **The home page is now a single search box.** At 69 tools the card grid was
  a long scroll to reach a tool you already knew by name. The full catalogue
  moved to `/all/`, and the "Pasang di situsmu" card folds away
- Reordered the roadmap by priority: everyday tools first (WhatsApp link,
  percent and discount, unit price, electricity tokens), and a new emergency
  category (SOS, medical card, CPR, first aid) moved up
- A "Traktir kopi" link in the footer, plus a small toast shown at most once
  per session, and only after a file was saved

**Lesson:** once a catalogue passes a few dozen items, people come with a name
in mind. A search box serves them better than any grid.

**Next:** the three everyday tools at the front of the roadmap.

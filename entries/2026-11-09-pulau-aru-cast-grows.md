# Pulau Aru: The Cast Grows

Episodes 20 to 24 of [Pulau Aru](https://pulau-aru.web.id/), still two a day.

- A second wave of six recurring residents (a presidential bodyguard, a fisherman,
  one who always borrows money, and others), with their own poses and props, and
  a rule for when older residents make cameos
- New buildings from requests: a workshop, a five-storey presidential apartment with
  a penthouse, and a hotel by the pier with a guest book and a queue at check-in
- A study tour: a teacher leads a group of residents around the island along a set
  route, with a banner
- More buildings opened inside: the food stall, the coffee shop, the pharmacy
- A first TikTok analytics snapshot, 23 videos, saved as CSV in the repo so later
  episodes can be compared against it

# VWO Job Fair: Charging the Side That Is Hiring

[jobfair.co.id](https://jobfair.co.id) cannot charge job seekers: placement rules in
Indonesia forbid taking money from people looking for work. Today's three pull
requests move the money to companies instead. Claude Code wrote the code and
tests; I set the rules for what is free, what is paid and who pays.

- **Diamonds, a company-only currency.** Companies search a candidate list with
  masked names and pay diamonds to open a full profile. Opening the same person
  again is free, and only people who agreed to be found ever appear
- I rewrote the consent text so it says exactly what a company sees, and stored
  which version each person agreed to. People on the old wording keep the old
  promise: no photo, no contact, only an in-app invitation to apply
- Job seekers get a CV builder that publishes an ATS-friendly page with a print
  layout. It costs coins, but the welcome coins already cover it, so nobody has to
  pay money for it
- The blue check is now earned (a complete profile with a CV, a month of activity,
  applications to three companies) instead of bought with coins
- Organisers can import their old site's job seekers from a spreadsheet. Profiles
  attach when the same person signs in, and the owner sees "registered" and
  "signed in" as separate numbers
- Rented buildings got stands, rent invoices, a monthly revenue split and a place
  in the park

**Lesson:** if one side of a marketplace cannot be charged, make the paid action
the one only the other side needs, and keep everything the free side needs
reachable without money.

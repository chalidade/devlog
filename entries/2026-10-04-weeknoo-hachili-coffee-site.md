# weeknoo: A Site for a Bee-House Cafe

A new site in [weeknoo](https://chalidade.github.io/weeknoo/) for Ha.Chi.Li Coffee,
a cafe in Singosari, Malang. I scaffolded it from the restaurant category, then
rebuilt it around the cafe's own brand.

- Orange and honey palette, a honeycomb pattern, Saira with Plus Jakarta Sans, and
  an SVG redraw of the cafe's two-hexagon bee logo
- The full menu book as a section you can search, with tabs. A bee marks a crowd
  favourite and an orange card marks a signature item
- New menu drops, recommended combos, ambience, reviews, a location section with a
  map, and a WhatsApp button for event reservations
- Photos are crops from the cafe's own menu book, with no stock images
- Its card went into the homepage gallery

**Lesson:** a category template gives me the page structure for free. What makes
the site read as this cafe and no other is its own material: the logo, the
colours, the photos from its menu book.

# Tools: Unit Converter and Date Calculator

Day 5 of the [tools roadmap](https://github.com/chalidade/tools/blob/main/ROADMAP.md):
everyday arithmetic. Two tools in the productivity category, bringing the
[site](https://chalidade.github.io/tools/) to 54.

- **Unit converter:** length, weight (including ons and kuintal), area (are,
  hectare and tumbak), volume, temperature (Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin and
  Reamur), speed, and data with both decimal KB/MB and binary KiB/MiB. It
  accepts a decimal comma, has a swap button, and shows a table of the value in
  every unit at once
- **Date calculator:** exact age in years, months and days with a countdown to
  the next birthday, the difference between two dates, and adding or
  subtracting days, weeks, months, years or working days
- **Working days know Indonesian holidays.** National holidays and cuti bersama
  for 2025–2026 are bundled as JSON, so there's no outside API to fail

**Lesson:** the units people actually use (ons, tumbak, Reamur) are what make a
converter feel local. A generic list would have been easier to build and
nobody here would have used it.

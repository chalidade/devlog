# VWO Job Fair: The Tools a Company Actually Pays For

[jobfair.co.id](https://jobfair.co.id) is my virtual job fair. With payments in
coins working, the day went to what makes a stand worth buying for a company and
easier to run for the organiser: 20 PRs merged (#76 to #95).
Claude Code wrote the code; I chose the features and reviewed the screens.

- **Recruiting tools beyond the booth:** a talent pool with matching candidates
  per vacancy, Excel export and an ATS webhook, a post-event report per booth (in
  the portal and as PDF) that compares events, and numbered invoices with tax
  invoice data and bank transfer for coin packages
- A small-business package (Paket mini UMKM) and a featured-vacancy add-on, so a
  local shop can join for less than a full stand
- The organiser can move any stand to any floor and slot, or swap two; skills and
  domicile are picked from fixed lists so the data can be filtered
- For seekers: interview and seminar reminders, a seminar schedule with
  registration, invite links with a campus leaderboard, and random anomalies on
  the floors (a cat, fireflies) whose rewards the server decides
- The company and organiser dashboards went through three looks in one day,
  ending on a minimal monochrome one with a light mode

**Lesson:** a recruiter judges a job fair by what they take home afterwards. The
export, the report and the invoice sell the stand more than the stand itself.
